08 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

What services does Tantra Gen Tech provide?

We provide website and app development, Agentic AI, AI agents for business, AI chatbots, digital marketing support, and custom AI solutions.

Can you build a website and integrate AI into it?

Yes. We can explore a combined website and AI solution where it fits your requirements, user journey, and operational process.

What is the difference between an AI chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot primarily has conversations. An AI agent can be designed to reason through defined tasks and use approved tools or workflows, with safeguards and human oversight as needed.

Can you automate an existing business workflow?

We can review the workflow, identify appropriate automation opportunities, and scope a solution around your approved tools and processes.

Do you build custom applications?

Yes. We can build tailored web applications, dashboards, tools, and other digital products around a defined business need.

How is project pricing determined?

Pricing depends on scope, integrations, complexity, and requirements. A project discussion helps us understand the work before proposing an approach.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on the scope, features, feedback cycle, integrations, and technical complexity. These are discussed as part of planning.

Can you maintain and improve a solution after launch?

Yes. Ongoing improvements, refinements, and future enhancements can be considered after launch.

What information should I provide to start a project?

Share what you are trying to achieve, the current challenge, intended users, useful constraints, and any existing systems or timelines to consider.
